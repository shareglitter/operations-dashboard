Reading this tab
  • Source: the five opt-in flags on Subscribers (Snow, Leaves, TrashCan, Weeds, Compost), synced from ActiveCampaign tags. One filtered request returns only subscribers with at least one flag set.
  • This is interest, not revenue. Compost has shipped, so it is charted for reference only (lighter bar) and left out of the tiles — the Compost tab tracks its paying add-ons.
  • There is no date on the flags yet, so no trend over time. If ActiveCampaign can sync the tag date too, this tab gets a monthly series.
  • Zone / neighborhood splits would need the subscriber's zip or block zone on the proxy allow-list — easy to add once there is a question to answer.

Reading this tab
  • Source of truth is the Compost Add-On checkbox on Active Subscriptions, fetched with a server-side filter (one request, ~60 rows). Status buckets follow Contribution Status: Active · pending (Pledged, Not Yet Paying / Pledged, Block Not Active) · churned (everything else).
  • Interested, not added = the subscriber answered "Yes, add compost…" on the signup form (Compost Opt-In on Subscribers) but no subscription has the add-on ticked. That's the follow-up list.
  • Revenue is estimated at $15 / month per active add-on; the MRR field on subscriptions is the whole pledge, not compost alone.
  • Pilot blocks come from the Compost Pilot checkbox on Blocks. A block with add-ons but no pilot flag is identified but has no bin yet — expected during rollout.
  • Bin fill levels per pickup come from the vendor's log (section above), not Airtable. The two are joined on Block Code.
