What this tab needs
Today the only service-interest signal in Airtable is the Services multi-select on Subscribers, and its options are Cleaning and Compost. Nothing records interest in leaf, snow, weed or trash-can service yet, so this tab can't show it.
  • Lightest fix: add Leaves, Snow, Weeds, Trash cans as options on Services and expose them on the signup / account form. This chart picks up new options automatically.
  • If interest ≠ subscription: a small Service Interest table (subscriber, service, date, source, block) keeps "curious" separate from "paying", and gives a per-month series + a by-zone view.
  • Either way, the request is one filtered fetch; no refresh script changes.

Reading this tab
  • Source of truth is the Compost Add-On checkbox on Active Subscriptions, fetched with a server-side filter (one request, ~60 rows). Status buckets follow Contribution Status: Active · pending (Pledged, Not Yet Paying / Pledged, Block Not Active) · churned (everything else).
  • Interested, not added = the subscriber answered "Yes, add compost…" on the signup form (Compost Opt-In on Subscribers) but no subscription has the add-on ticked. That's the follow-up list.
  • Revenue is estimated at $15 / month per active add-on; the MRR field on subscriptions is the whole pledge, not compost alone.
  • Pilot blocks come from the Compost Pilot checkbox on Blocks; Compost Neighbors is the hand-kept email list there and is shown for comparison. A block with add-ons but no pilot flag is identified but has no bin yet — expected during rollout.
  • No bin-count, pickup-log or weight data exists yet. A Compost Pickups table (block, date, bins, est. lbs) would give this tab a diversion series like the ops page's bags chart.
